description=[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]
description=One of the important factors was the flexibility to use this in most environments. It was important to allow you to manage your loot and spawn rates plus configure the speed/strength of the stoned status.[b]
description=[list]
description=  [*]Loot Distribution is managed by (Loot Setting * Spawn Multiplier * rarity). Rarity can be found in the loot lua file but is between 1 and .01
description=
description=  [*]Stoned Increase and Decrease will specifically manage the value change amount over time. This is mostly to accommodate time settings. 1 hour standard fits the default 2 value. If you played a 2 hour setting then 4 would be best. In the end its up to you
description=
description=  [*]Munchies multiplier impacts the amount changes to thirst and hunger stats while under the stoned status
description=
description=  [*]Stoner Perk and Paranoid options impact the custom trait influences while under the stoned status
description=
description=  [*]Rare magazines are for stronger variants. These are meant to be read only once so they will be removed from your inventory after learning the recipe
description=
description=  [*]Ability to change the amount of harvest gained
description=
description=  [*]Influence the time it takes to grow, rot, or dry, these settings will require a restart and will only impact new items
description=
description=  [*]NEW OPTION to turn off natural spawns of tobacco or cannabis items. This allows for removing cannabis and keeping only tobacco or the opposite of only cannabis and no tobacco. It influences the items in the smokes kit too.
description=[/list]
